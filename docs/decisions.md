# Decisions

## 2026-10-17 — 20-place picker

Decision: Ship the 20-place picker with the limits above stated in the README. Keep the risk thresholds as they are. Soil pH and "best area to plant" stay out.

Reason: Coordinates are town centres and the forecast is grid-based, so a place is an approximate zone at coffee altitude. The thresholds have no PROCAFÉ or agronomist validation yet (the app footer already says so). Moving them to balance the bands would hide a real signal; alto share is reported as measured.
